Privacy Policy

How Savara Club collects, uses and protects your information — in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

01Who we are

Savara Club (“Savara”, “we”, “us” or “our”) operates this website and the nightclub & dining venue located at Anjuna Caisua, 631/2, Ozran Beach Rd, Vagator, Goa 403509, India. This policy explains how we handle your personal data when you visit our website, book a table, purchase tickets, or attend our venue.

02Information we collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact details — name, email address and phone number you provide via our forms or reservations.
  • Booking & ticketing data — event, date, number of guests and related preferences.
  • Payment information — processed in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹) by our third-party payment gateways; we do not store your full card details on our servers.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device information and pages visited, collected via cookies and analytics.

03How we use your information

We use your personal data to:

  • process reservations, ticket purchases and payments in INR;
  • send booking confirmations, updates and, where you have opted in, marketing about events and offers;
  • operate, secure and improve our website and services;
  • comply with legal, tax and regulatory obligations in India.

04Legal basis & your consent

We process your data on the basis of your consent, the performance of a contract (such as a booking), and our legitimate interests in running the venue. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time by contacting us.

05Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how the site is used. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; some features may not function correctly without them.

06Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with trusted service providers who help us operate — such as payment gateways, ticketing partners, email and analytics providers — and with authorities where required by Indian law.

07Payments

All transactions on this website are processed in Indian Rupees (₹). Payments are handled by PCI-DSS compliant Indian payment gateways. Prices displayed include applicable taxes unless stated otherwise.

08Data retention

We keep your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described here, including to satisfy legal, accounting or reporting requirements under Indian law, after which it is securely deleted or anonymised.

09Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • request correction or completion of inaccurate data;
  • request erasure of your data, subject to legal retention requirements;
  • withdraw consent and lodge a grievance with us or the Data Protection Board of India.

10Data security

We implement reasonable security practices and procedures as required under the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices) Rules, 2011 to protect your data against unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure.

11Age restriction

Entry to Savara Club is restricted to guests aged 18 and above, in accordance with local regulations in Goa. This website and our services are not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
