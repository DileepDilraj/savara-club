About Savara Club: born from fire.

Savara is a premium nightclub experience where live music, immersive light and performance rise like a phoenix — carrying the dance floor until dawn.

Our story

A cathedral of sound since 2023

What began as a single warehouse night has grown into a home for the world’s most electrifying artists. We built Savara around one idea: energy should be constructive — it should lift you, not burn you out.

From precision-tuned sound to fire performers suspended above the floor, every detail is engineered so you leave feeling reborn.
